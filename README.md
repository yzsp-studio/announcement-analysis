- 检索取证按六类目标＋联动检索＋股价阶段表现检查推进
- 不适用于不依赖公告原文的行业研究或公司深度报告，那类任务走 company-analysis / industry-analysis

## 🇬🇧 English

Searches and interprets listed-company announcements across three markets: A-shares (Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing), Hong Kong (HKEX) and the US (SEC EDGAR). It runs in two modes: deep reading of one or several specific announcements — breaking out key points, judging materiality and giving multi-angle analysis — or batch monitoring by company or date range that produces a digest with freshness and coverage first. It covers earnings reports, major contract and agreement announcements, equity changes (increases, decreases, buybacks), equity incentives (options, restricted stock, employee stock ownership plans), regulatory inquiry and concern letters with replies, trading suspension/resumption and delisting-risk announcements, plus batch tracking of recent filings. It is not meant for industry or company deep-dive research that does not rest on the announcement texts themselves.

### ✨ What it does

- Searches announcements across A-shares (Shanghai/Shenzhen/Beijing), HKEX and SEC EDGAR
- Deep-read mode: breaks out key points, judges materiality and gives multi-angle analysis
- Batch monitor mode: tracks by company or date range and outputs a digest with freshness and coverage first
- Six focused categories: earnings, major contracts, equity changes, equity incentives, regulatory inquiries and replies, suspension/resumption and delisting risk
- Evidence gathering follows six targets plus linked searches and a stock-price phase check
- Excludes industry research and company deep dives that do not depend on announcement texts
